## Hi there 👋
The thesis focuses on developing a proposed hybrid model for analyzing tuberculosis chest X-rays, a critical area in medical imaging due to the rising prevalence of tuberculosis globally. This topic was chosen to address the need for more accurate and efficient diagnostic tools that can assist healthcare professionals in early detection and treatment planning. The research will be conducted by utilizing various datasets, including those from Qatar University, MC, Shenzhen, and Pakistan, and applying advanced techniques such as data preprocessing, noise removal, and data augmentation through DCGAN.
